The Neon Is Still On.
No One's Left To Turn It Off.

Sun Coast City was a monument to corporate ambition. Towers of glass and signal, a population of millions wired into a system that promised everything and delivered enough to keep them quiet. Then the corporations left. Then the power started going out in sectors. Then the dead stopped staying dead.

Something is using the city's infrastructure. Its fiber lines, its drainage systems, its old transit tunnels. It doesn't think. It builds. And what it builds looks disturbingly intentional.

You are one of the last people running. Every card you play, every run you survive, pulls you deeper into what the city has become. Closer to understanding what it's trying to become next.
